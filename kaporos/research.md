### B3. The prayer and the Temple — HIGH
- Text (Chabad siddur): opens with Psalm 107:10, 14, 17–21 and Job 33:23–24, then three times: "This is my exchange, this is my substitute, this is my atonement (zeh chalifati, zeh temurati, zeh kapparati). This rooster shall go to its death and I shall proceed to a good, long life and peace."
  https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/320228/jewish/Text-of-Kapparot-in-Hebrew-and-English.htm
- Origin: Geonic Babylonia (7th–11th c.). The link to the Yom Kippur scapegoat (Lev. 16:5–10, *se'ir la'azazel*) is the common rabbinic and scholarly explanation, not stated in the Geonic texts themselves (MEDIUM). Say "echoes," not "is." "Gever" means both man and rooster.
  https://daytonjewishobserver.org/2014/09/kapparot/
- Liturgy asking for restored sacrifice: Amidah blessing 17 (*Retzeh*), three times daily: "restore the service to the Holy of Holies of Your House, and the fire-offerings of Israel." Sabbath and festival Musaf: "the Musaf offering of this day we will prepare and offer before You in love ... as You wrote for us in Your Torah," followed by Num. 28:9–10. Yom Kippur Musaf *Avodah* re-enacts the High Priest's Temple service.
  https://www.jewishencyclopedia.com/articles/1398-amidah
  https://opensiddur.org/shared/prayers/solilunar/shabbat/shabbat-musaf/
