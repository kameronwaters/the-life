# Grok brief — kaporos-part3 (comparison to the in-house render)

Written in the style of a friend's design brief (full-background painting + presenter cutout + stacked title +
brand palette + "make four designs"), re-pointed at this video and the Christspiracy colour ways.

---

Let's make a premium YouTube thumbnail. Design this thumbnail like you are a world class thumbnail designer. Do not do the regular AI thumbnail style. Format is 16:9 landscape, 1280 x 720 pixels. Title on the thumbnail reads "HE'S THE MESSIAH!?"

I am uploading three images. Image 1 is me, a selfie shot on the ground at the event. Image 2 is a documentary frame of a Hasidic man in a black hat swinging a live white chicken over his head while reading from a prayer book, which will be the full background. Image 3 is a real sticker photographed on a Brooklyn street that reads "MESSIAH IS HERE" with the face of the late Lubavitcher Rebbe.

This video is the third and final part of a documentary on Kaporos, the chicken sacrifice performed on Brooklyn sidewalks the night before Yom Kippur. I filmed the ritual up close: thousands of birds in crates, men swinging them overhead while saying "this is my substitute, this is my atonement", then the slaughter. Then I found out the community doing it believes their dead rabbi is the Messiah, that his headquarters is the Third Temple in waiting, and that the end of their story is a rebuilt altar in Jerusalem with the knives out again. The thumbnail's job is to tie the man swinging the chicken to the Messiah claim, with me there as the witness. The title of the video carries the cult angle, so the thumbnail must not repeat it; it only needs the swing, the sticker and the two-line question. Tone is serious, investigative, on-the-ground, not mocking, not preachy.

Background: use image 2 as the full-frame background. Keep the frame's composition, figures and details as they are. Do not redraw or replace the man, the hat, the prayer book or the chicken. The most important part of the frame is the chicken held aloft by its legs, wings open, top right of frame. Keep the bird and the raised arm clearly visible and uncovered. Grade the frame to fit a moody documentary look: deepen the shadows, cool the blacks very slightly, keep skin and feathers natural, add a vignette, and darken the top-left third of the frame so the title stands out. It must still look like a photograph, not an illustration.

Foreground: place me from image 1 to the right of centre in the lower half of the frame, cut out cleanly from the chest up, lit to match the overcast daylight of the frame so I look like I am standing in the scene. Do not cover the chicken or the man's face. Keep my face exactly the same, a 100% accurate representation of the picture, upscaled to premium quality. Place image 3, the sticker, small on the right side at the height of my shoulder, slightly rotated, as if stuck on the plywood behind me, with the same grade as everything else.

Title placement: stack the title in two lines ("HE'S THE" / "MESSIAH!?") in the top-left of the frame over the darkened area, "MESSIAH!?" the larger line and the only coloured word. Keep my face and all title text inside the safe area away from the bottom-right corner where YouTube places the duration badge. The title must still read when the thumbnail is 168 pixels wide in the sidebar.

Brand colours to pull from: White #FFFFFF, Brand Red #E45428 (for the one key word only), Cream #ECE0C4, Gold #F7E9A8, Ink #21211F, Black #000000. No other colours in the type. Typography should be heavy condensed caps like PP Formula Condensed Black or Anton, white with a hard black drop shadow, red for the one key word. One design may set "Messiah!?" in a heavy serif italic like PP Eiko Black Italic as the accent word. No other text on the image besides the title. No arrows, circles, emoji, eyebrows or straplines.

Make four designs.
