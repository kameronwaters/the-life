# Prompt for Claude (attach the 8 files in assets/claude_pack/ in order)

You are a world-class key-art designer who has done A24 one-sheets and the best YouTube thumbnails of the last five years. I need one 16:9 YouTube thumbnail (1280x720, deliver at 2560x1440) for the final part of my Kaporos documentary. Do not give me the generic AI-thumbnail look. Think retro painted film poster meets Soviet constructivist photo-collage meets renaissance chiaroscuro, finished like a modern A24 poster.

1. **Me (image 1).** Studio photo, 4K. This is the presenter. My face, beard, eyes, mouth, expression, the cream cap that reads MAKE CHRISTIANS VEGAN AGAIN, the black CHRISTSPIRACY tank and the handheld mic are all locked. Treat the face as a photograph you may relight and upscale but never repaint, caricature, slim, age or beautify. If the face drifts even slightly from image 1, the design fails.
2. **The rabbi (image 2).** The chicken-swinging man, 4K. Same rule: face, white beard, black hat, raised arm, prayer book and the live white chicken are locked. This is the hero gesture of the image.
3. **The sticker (image 3).** The real MESSIAH IS HERE sticker with the Rebbe's portrait. Keep it readable. Put it on a street-light pole, a wall, or pasted like a torn poster fragment.
4. **The crowd (image 4).** A real photo of thousands of Chabad-Lubavitch shluchim in black fedoras in front of 770 Eastern Parkway. Use it, or the crowd from it, as the background world. Simplify and darken it so the two men pop.
5. **Style reference: The Surfer (2024) poster.** Sun-bleached orange and cream, brushed texture, a flat circular sun behind the figure, warm single light, grounded realism.
6. **Style reference: 1920s Soviet Jewish cinema poster.** Cream paper, a huge flat black graphic shape, photographic cutout of a man in a hat, sharp red geometric accents, printed halftone.
7. **Style reference: painted Godfather poster.** Painterly realism, soft gradient sky, face rendered as fine art, landscape below.
8. **My own footage frame of the rabbi (image 8).** Same man, lower quality; use only if you need a second angle of his face.

## The story the image must tell

Part three of a documentary on Kaporos, the chicken sacrifice performed on Brooklyn sidewalks the night before Yom Kippur. Thousands of birds, men swinging them overhead while reciting "this is my substitute, this is my atonement," then the slaughter. The community doing it believes their late Rebbe is the Messiah. The image is: the man swinging the chicken, the Messiah sticker, a sea of black hats behind, and me as the shocked witness. The video title carries the cult angle, so the thumbnail only needs those three ideas and the two-line question.

## Composition (non-negotiable)

- Me on the RIGHT, chest up, face large, turned to camera, the clear focal point.
- The rabbi centre-left, slightly behind me, the chicken rising toward the top edge. Nothing covers the bird, his face or my face.
- The sticker small between us or on the pole at the right edge.
- The crowd and 770 as a simplified, darkened midground.
- Leave the upper-left third clean and calm for the title. Give me one version with NO text at all, and one with the title set as two stacked lines, "HE'S THE" in white and "MESSIAH!?" larger in red #E45428, heavy condensed grotesk caps like PP Formula Condensed Black or Anton, hard black drop shadow. No other words, no arrows, no circles, no emoji.
- Everything inside the safe area away from the bottom-right corner, and it must still read at 168 pixels wide.

## Look

- One unified light: warm sodium/tungsten key from upper right, cool blue-black shadows, haze in front of the crowd, rim light on hair and beard. Chiaroscuro like Caravaggio, restrained like A24.
- Surface: painted-poster texture and halftone grain over the background and clothes, but faces stay photographic and sharp. Fine paper grain, vignette, slight misregistration if you go constructivist.
- Palette only: burnt orange, cream #ECE0C4, chocolate brown, black, white, and one note of red #E45428.

## Deliverables

Four distinct designs: (a) painted retro poster with the Surfer sun-halo behind the rabbi, (b) Soviet photo-collage with the black silhouette crowd and a red diagonal, (c) chiaroscuro night-street realism, (d) your own best idea. Each as a text-free plate plus a titled version. Before you show them, check every face against images 1 and 2 and tell me honestly if any drifted.
